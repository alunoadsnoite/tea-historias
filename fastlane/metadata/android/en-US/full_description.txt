TEA Histórias offers nine illustrated social stories about everyday situations, such as visiting the dentist, taking the bus, and asking for a break. Read one scene at a time and pick up where you left off.

Create stories with your own text and illustrations from the collection. Reorder scenes, search by title or category, and mark your favorite stories.

The interface and included stories are available in Portuguese, English, Spanish, and French. You can enlarge the text, hide illustrations, and choose a light, dark, or automatic theme.

The library and editor work offline. Stories, preferences, and reading progress stay on your device. The contact link opens your Android email app.
